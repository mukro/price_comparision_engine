04 / B2B Merchant Engine

The Merchant Command Center

PCE doesn't just show prices — it helps merchants win on price, automatically and safely.

⚙ Dynamic Repricing Rules

Merchants configure rules like "undercut Amazon by Rs. 50, but never below my 15% margin floor." PCE monitors competitor prices every 5 minutes and calculates the optimal counter-price instantly.

  • Undercut by fixed amount or percentage
  • Match competitor exactly
  • Respect MAP (Minimum Advertised Price) rules
  • Margin floor protection

🛡 Circuit Breaker Safety

A bad scrape or bug could crash a merchant's price to zero. The circuit breaker caps daily price drops at 10%, preventing catastrophic losses. Think of it as an airbag for pricing.

  • 24-hour rolling window monitoring
  • Auto-caps price velocity
  • Full audit trail of every decision
  • Merchant override capability

🔗 Auto Webhook Integration

When a competitor drops price, PCE evaluates the merchant's rules and instantly POSTs a recommended price to the merchant's e-commerce platform via secure webhook. Zero manual intervention.

  • Real-time webhook dispatch
  • JSON payload with reasoning
  • Auto-apply or manual review mode
  • Retry logic with exponential backoff

📝 Audit & Compliance

Every price change, every scrape, every decision is logged. Merchants and admins get full transparency into why a price was set — essential for legal and financial audits.

  • Immutable price audit logs
  • Scraping compliance dashboard
  • robots.txt & domain allowlist checks
  • Rate limiting per domain

06 / AI & Machine Learning

The Intelligence Layer

PCE isn't just a database — it's a learning system that gets smarter with every price point.

🤖

Buy Timing Prediction

Analyzes 90 days of historical price data to tell users "BUY NOW" or "WAIT" with confidence percentages. Uses statistical analysis (min, avg, median, percentile) — no black box. Transparent reasoning included.

🔌

Spec-Equivalent Alternative Finder

Uses 384-dimensional vector embeddings (Sentence-Transformers) to find cheaper products with 75%+ spec similarity. Cross-brand recommendations that actually make sense.

🔍

Hybrid Search (RRF)

Combines full-text search (PostgreSQL tsvector) with vector similarity (pgVector cosine distance) using Reciprocal Rank Fusion. Results are both keyword-accurate and semantically relevant.

📋

OCR Receipt Intelligence

Google ML Kit on-device OCR extracts invoice IDs, amounts, dates, and vendor names from physical receipts. No cloud OCR costs. Works offline. Privacy-first.

⚙

Dynamic Pricing AI (B2B)

Evaluates merchant rules against live competitor data, applies circuit breakers, and recommends optimal prices. The AI respects human-set guardrails — it optimizes within boundaries, never overrides them.

AI-Proofing Strategy: Don't fight AI — become infrastructure AI depends on. Build PCE AI API for Perplexity/ChatGPT to call. Build merchant AI co-pilot for dynamic pricing. Focus on proprietary data AI can't replicate: conversion attribution, partner relationships, 90-day price history.
